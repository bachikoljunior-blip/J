# AGI root problem — problem tree

Updated: 2026-08-19 17:24 JST

## Root
Deliver AGI without lowering the achievement criteria, not merely as a prototype/research artifact, but in a form that is actually usable.

AGI itself is distinct from this problem-solving mechanism. Achievement must not be claimed without empirical evidence.

## Problem-count prediction and mandatory rewrite history
The initial forecast was 31 active parent+child problems. A first decomposition exceeded it, triggering Tree v2 and a reforecast of 36. Tree v2 reached exactly 36 active non-replaced problems.

On 2026-08-19 17:23 JST, X2.1b1 was attempted directly. The signed admission machinery existed, but Issue #16 still had zero expressions of interest and no real outside custodian could be counted. The attempt exposed three necessary evidence stages: obtain real participation, externally qualify independence/conflicts/capability, and obtain complete two-lineage family coverage. Appending these as ordinary children would raise the active count above 36, so the required second transversal rewrite is triggered.

The second rewrite uses a cross-cutting **independent evidence federation** rather than adding recruitment-only branches. The low-probability/high-leverage move is to make one independently governed federation supply three things that were previously scattered across X2 and X3: sealed task custody, competent-human calibration, and independent rerun/security observation. Each admitted federation member can contribute one or more evaluator capabilities, but independence is measured at the legal/control/signing/implementation lineage level and cannot be manufactured by labels. This reduces duplicated external-participant acquisition, identity/conflict auditing, custody, scheduling, and rerun machinery without weakening any AGI gate.

Reforecast total active non-replaced parent+child problems: **34**.

## Acceptance contract (unchanged)
An AGI claim is accepted only if all gates pass on the frozen candidate: generality across preregistered required domains, competent-human-level performance in every required family and aggregate, long-horizon autonomous decomposition/tool use/error recovery/state preservation, practical third-party delivery of the exact evaluated artifact, and anti-gaming/contamination controls with independent statistically meaningful rerun. Failures cannot be averaged away and unverifiable or materially contaminated evidence is invalidated rather than silently substituted.
