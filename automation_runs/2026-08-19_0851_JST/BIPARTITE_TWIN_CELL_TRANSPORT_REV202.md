## Existing-world / cross-layer check

At the graph-structure layer, an exact twin class is a module whose vertices have identical opposite-side incidence and identical stored color. At the group layer, once source and target twin classes are paired, the freedom internal to the paired classes is the direct product of full symmetric groups on those classes. At the coset layer, this means one representative cellwise bijection multiplied by the target-cell setwise stabilizer generated by independent transpositions. This observation simultaneously removes a graph-level ambiguity and packages the entire family into the repository's existing Schreier/right-coset substrate rather than enumerating factorially many mappings.

No external theorem conclusion is used to assert progress here: completeness is checked mechanically from the exact twin definition, the generated subgroup order, and the repository's explicit right-coset convention.

## Implemented progress

`bipartite_twin_cell_transport_coset_v1.py`:

- accepts only rev201 `exact_unique_twin_quotient_mapping` results;
- constructs a whole-domain representative mapping every paired left/right twin cell positionwise;
- generates the full symmetric group independently on every **target** twin cell using adjacent transpositions;
- verifies the Schreier subgroup order equals the exact product of cell factorials;
- verifies every subgroup generator preserves its target cell and that representative-followed-by-generator transports lie in the returned `RightCoset`;
- returns `exact_complete_twin_cell_transport_coset` only after these checks;
- remains fail closed on ambiguous rev201 quotient mappings.

The regression exhausts the 9! ambient permutations for a small case and confirms that right-coset membership has exactly the expected eight cellwise transports and no extras; every member maps the source bipartite graph to the target graph.
