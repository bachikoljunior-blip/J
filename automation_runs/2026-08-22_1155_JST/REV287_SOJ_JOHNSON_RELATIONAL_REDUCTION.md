# AGI-GI rev287 — exact larger-ground Johnson relational reduction construction

## Scope

This revision fills only the construction obligation that rev291's larger-ground recursive handoff deliberately leaves caller-supplied. Given a certified full `J(v,k)` embedding of the represented action and generators on that Johnson vertex domain, rev287 reconstructs the Johnson ground from point-incidence stars and emits replayable reduction evidence compatible with the rev291 handoff contract.

The operator verifies all of the following before certification:

- `v >= 4`, `2 <= k <= v-2`, and `C(v,k) > v`, so the auxiliary action measure strictly shrinks;
- the supplied vertex embedding is a bijection onto every `k`-subset of the named ground;
- ground points are reconstructed from their complete incidence-star signatures and relabeled deterministically by those signatures;
- every supplied ambient generator is a genuine permutation of all `C(v,k)` Johnson vertices;
- every generator maps every reconstructed point-incidence star to another point-incidence star;
- the induced ground permutation agrees with the ambient generator on every Johnson vertex, not merely on sampled relations;
- the `v=2k` complement automorphism fails closed because it maps stars to non-star maximum cliques rather than being silently promoted to a ground permutation.

The certified object exposes the exact fields consumed by rev291: `status=certified_johnson_ground_relational_reduction`, canonical/exact/progress flags, exact solution and ambient-membership transport flags, explicit complement handling, source/ground/child measures, deterministic multiplicity `1`, and a canonical SHA-256 reduction identity. The identity binds the complete canonical vertex-to-subset relation, reconstructed stars, ambient generators, induced ground generators, and a deterministic construction-work bound.

## Exactness argument

A coloring on the Johnson action is equivalently a coloring on the verified family of all `k`-subsets. Once each ambient generator is proven to equal the set action of its induced ground permutation on every vertex, this correspondence commutes with every generator and therefore with every group element generated by them. Thus source-solution transport and ambient subgroup membership transport are exact without enumerating the group. Faithfulness follows from the full `k`-subset action in the admitted range `2 <= k <= v-2`.

The reduction is deterministic and has one recursive child, so its multiplicative branch cost is exactly `1.0`. The recorded polynomial construction-work bound is not promoted into a global recurrence theorem here; rev291 remains responsible for consuming separately certified cost/accounting evidence.
