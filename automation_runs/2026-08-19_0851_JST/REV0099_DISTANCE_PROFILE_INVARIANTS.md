rev99 assigns each vertex an exact integer signature consisting of its persistent attribute and, for every exact attribute bucket, the multiset of shortest-path distances to all vertices in that bucket (including `-1` for disconnected vertices). Any exact attributed-graph isomorphism must preserve this signature. Therefore, when the joint signature inventory matches and every signature class is singleton, the induced mapping is forced by an exact graph invariant rather than by an arbitrary choice. The complete attribute and adjacency matrices are still directly verified before release.
