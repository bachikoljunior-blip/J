# Independent evidence federation — participation protocol

This protocol supports F1 of the AGI problem tree. It does **not** claim that any named organization participates and does not weaken the AGI acceptance contract.

## Roles a participant may offer

A genuinely independent participant may offer one or more capabilities:

- sealed task-generator custody/implementation for specified preregistered families;
- competent-human recruitment/calibration for specified families;
- independently custodied rerun execution;
- autonomy/security observation or adversarial-trial operation.

A participant covering multiple roles is useful operationally, but one entity never counts as multiple independent lineages merely because it has multiple teams or labels.

## Minimum public expression of interest

Only non-secret metadata should be public:

1. stable organization/custodian identity;
2. offered roles and domains/families;
3. ability to preserve non-public custody through final rerun;
4. willingness to provide externally auditable identity/conflict/control evidence;
5. stable signing principal;
6. private contact route.

Do not publish task generators, tasks, answers, private templates, seed schedules, credentials, private keys, or other secret evaluation material.

## Qualification gate

Before any coverage is counted, the evaluator must verify:

- the participant is a real outside person/organization rather than a project-controlled alias;
- legal/control relationships and material conflicts are disclosed and externally auditable;
- candidate developers cannot access final private evaluation content;
- implementation lineage for a sealed bank does not share private generator material with its peer lineage;
- the signing principal controls an accepted key and signs the frozen participation declaration;
- claimed role/family capability has concrete evidence sufficient for the assigned role;
- the participant accepts fail-closed invalidation for material leakage, candidate-specific tuning, false independence claims, or custody failure.

A signature proves control of a signing key over a declaration. It does not, by itself, prove independence or truth of the declaration.

## Coverage rule

The frozen federation matrix must satisfy every preregistered requirement. Each final task family requires at least two genuinely independent sealed-generator custody/implementation lineages. Human calibration, independent rerun, and security/autonomy observation must also have explicitly assigned independent participants. The evaluator must reject documentary relabeling that would cause the same legal/control lineage to be counted twice.

## Private handoff

After public expression of interest, identity/conflict evidence and secret evaluation artifacts move to private custody channels. The public repository retains only commitments, digests, qualification outcomes, invalidation events, and other metadata that does not reveal final task content.

## Current evidence status

This document defines the protocol only. It is not evidence that a real federation participant has been accepted. F1.1 remains open until real external participation and qualification exist.
